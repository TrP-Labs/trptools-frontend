Only upload images you own or are licensed to use, and make sure they are

## Moderation

Anyone signed in can report a group, route, depot or image. A report hides the
reported item straight away — waiting for a human would leave genuinely abusive
content in front of the public in the meantime. A site administrator then
either clears it, which restores it and exempts it from being hidden by later
reports, or leaves it hidden.

Site administrators may also suspend an account temporarily or permanently
where these terms have been broken. A suspended account cannot sign in, and any
API keys it holds stop working. A temporary suspension lifts by itself; a
permanent one stays until an administrator removes it.
